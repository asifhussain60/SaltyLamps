## Material false positives and assumptions corrected

1. **Step names exceeded the proven scope.** Steps 1 and 2 now close only owner/destination identity and the single verified local preparation copy. Outstanding permissions and missing source material are not silently marked complete. The browser's personal checkbox ticks are separate from the reviewed stage status. Because checklist items were added and reordered, the browser uses a new storage key; old personal ticks remain stored under the previous key and cannot silently attach to different checks.
2. **The local postcode pass was mocked.** `tests/specs/order-review.spec.js` intercepts suggestions unless `LIVE_POSTCODE` is set. With that flag, the test failed waiting for the first suggestion. `wrangler d1 execute --local` addressed a local database with two test rows; `wrangler pages dev --d1` used a different local database containing the shop fixture but no postcode table. The earlier manual-entry handoff still passed; no actual suggestion service has been verified in the browser runtime. The two rows were added to the command-line local database only, never remotely. Do not treat the failed test as a production defect until a disposable Pages database is deliberately bootstrapped and retested.
3. **The DNS exit gate was stale.** The public website now serves an approved holding page. Zoho MX continuity is record-level evidence; actual incoming, outgoing and reply behavior belongs to step 8.
4. **One test count was stale.** The complete local unit suite now passes 151 checks, rather than the previously recorded 148. This is unit evidence, not full acceptance.
5. **Redirect rules need launch validation.** Local Pages startup warned that the absolute-source rule and catch-all rewrite were ignored. Cloudflare's [Pages redirects documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/configuration/redirects/) says a source must be a path and `_redirects` does not apply to Function-handled requests. Treat redirects as unproven until corrected and tested on the exact candidate release.
6. **Free-tier feasibility is conditional.** The current [D1 pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/) includes 100,000 writes per day and 5 GB total storage; [D1 limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/) cap each Free database at 500 MB. The postcode import must measure row count, database size, statement size and daily writes before deciding how to populate it. [Workers Free](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/) allows 100,000 requests per day and 10 ms CPU per request; usage and checkout performance are not yet measured. [R2 Standard](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/) has monthly free allowances but still requires owner billing opt-in. [Resend Free](https://resend.com/pricing) offers 3,000 monthly and 100 daily sends; two messages for a normal order and later notifications consume that allowance. Pricing and limits are not a guarantee of zero future cost.
7. **Policy and outstanding-order obligations needed explicit checks.** The launch checklist now calls out current policy wording, consent/marketing behavior, promotions, voucher or gift-card liabilities, pending refunds and business contact details. Those are owner decisions, not facts established by exporting settings.
