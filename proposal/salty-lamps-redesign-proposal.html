The bounce problem — specifically

A visitor lands and sees: a logo, the text "Energize Your Decor", an image, and a product grid. There is no story, no offer, no trust signal, no urgency. The brain classifies the page as "not useful" in under 3 seconds and leaves. That single pattern is the root of the entire traffic problem.

Homepage & the 3-Click Journey

Every section has a single job. Visitors go from "who are you?" to "add to cart" in three clicks.

Design principle: Lead with a feeling, not a product. The first thing a visitor sees must answer "why does this matter to me?" — not "here are some lamps for sale." Sensory language, ambient warmth, and a single unmistakable CTA do more conversion work than any product grid.

Click 1 — Hero → Collection

"Shop Salt Lamps" button on the hero lands the visitor directly in the most popular category. One action, one destination, no dead ends.

Click 2 — Card → Product Detail

Gallery, health benefits, variant selector, delivery promise, offer badge, and Add to Cart — everything needed to decide, on one screen.

Click 3 — Add to Cart → Checkout

Cart drawer with Apple Pay / Google Pay one-tap. Guest checkout — no forced account. Free shipping threshold visible immediately.

How Google Ranks Sites & What We're Doing About It

Google's ranking follows an exact logic you can engineer for. Here is how it works and how every decision in this build responds to it.

Google's 4-level ranking hierarchy

Level 1 — Can Google find & crawl it?

Sitemap · robots.txt · fast load · no broken pages

Level 2 — Does it match search intent?

Keyword relevance · content depth · structured data · unique URLs

Level 3 — Does Google trust it?

E-E-A-T · backlinks · GBP · reviews · real business signals

Level 4 — Do users actually like it?

Dwell time · scroll depth · return visits · low bounce · Core Web Vitals

Google Business Profile — Week 1

Free. Takes 5–7 days to verify by post. Immediately improves local and national visibility. Add products, post weekly. This single action is the fastest ranking win available before any code is written.

Structured Data — every page

JSON-LD on every page: Product, AggregateRating, Organization, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, Article. Rich snippets in Google results = higher click-through rate at the same ranking position.

Core Web Vitals targets

LCP < 2.5s · INP < 200ms · CLS < 0.1. Sites failing these lose −19% to −31% traffic vs faster competitors. React + Vite + Vercel CDN achieves these on launch day.

Organic Traffic — The Complete Strategy

No paid ads. Growth from four compounding channels: content, social, email, backlinks. Each feeds the others.

Organic-only is the right call. A well-written article about "salt walls for spas UK" will rank #1 within 60 days and send free trade enquiries forever. No ad budget replicates that compounding effect. Paid ads are documented in data/marketing-research.json as a future Phase 5 option once conversion data exists.

Content & Blog Strategy

12 articles/year minimum. Each targets a specific long-tail term and links to a product page.

  • Month 1: "Do Himalayan salt lamps actually work?"
  • Month 2: "Salt walls for therapy rooms — buyer's guide" (B2B, very low competition)
  • Month 3: "Himalayan salt lamp for sleep"
  • Month 4: "Culinary Himalayan salt vs table salt"
  • Month 5: "How we make our salt lamps — mine to home" (E-E-A-T goldmine)
  • Month 6: "Best Himalayan salt lamp UK guide 2026"
Email Marketing (42:1 ROI)

Captured via homepage lead magnet (free Salt Care Guide PDF).

  • Welcome sequence: 3 emails — story, bestsellers, health guide
  • Abandoned cart: 3 emails at 1hr, 24hr, 72hr
  • Post-purchase: delivery confirmation → review request → upsell
  • Seasonal: Christmas, Valentine's, Mother's Day
  • B2B newsletter: trade list, bulk pricing updates
Instagram & Pinterest

Salt lamps are among the most visually compelling products for social. Pinterest pins have 120-day organic life vs 24hr on Facebook.

  • Instagram: 3 posts/week — lifestyle, Reels of lamp glow (highly shareable)
  • Pinterest: 5 pins/week — home décor, wellness, gift boards
  • UGC: tag @saltylamps for 10% off next order
Backlink Building
  • Trade directories: wellness, spa, equestrian, food — each is a free backlink
  • Manufacturer story: pitch mine-to-UK angle to wellness press
  • Guest posts: write for wellness and home décor blogs
  • Partnerships: spa/yoga studio case studies earn links
  • Google Business Profile: generates citation link and domain trust

Modern Tech Stack

Every choice justified by SEO performance, developer velocity, and long-term maintainability.

Commerce engine TBD. Built behind src/lib/commerce.js abstraction. Critical requirement: must support automatic discount rules (Buy 1 Get 1 Half Price, Buy 2 Get 1 Free, Buy 3 Get 1 Free). Shopify handles this natively. WooCommerce requires Dynamic Pricing plugin (~£79/yr). This should be the deciding factor.

React 18 + Vite

Component UI, code-splitting, sub-2s page loads. SEO-critical: fast bundle = better Core Web Vitals = higher Google ranking.

Tailwind CSS v4

Custom salt-brand design tokens. Purged CSS bundle. Consistent layout system eliminates the column-shift bug of the current site.

Commerce Layer (TBD)

Shopify Headless or WooCommerce. Must support: automatic discount rules, Google Shopping feed, multi-currency.

React Router v6

Clean SEO-friendly URLs. Lazy-loaded routes. One consistent header/footer component — eliminates icon-shift bug permanently.

Sanity CMS

Non-developer editable blog, product copy, trade brochures. Enables the content strategy without a developer for every post.

Cloudinary

WebP auto-conversion, responsive srcsets, lazy loading. Existing product photos upload directly. Critical for LCP score.

JSON-LD Schema

Product, AggregateRating, Organization, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, Article on every applicable page type. Rich snippets in Google = higher CTR.

GA4 + Search Console

Track dwell time, scroll depth, conversion funnel. Search Console shows exactly which queries bring visitors — critical for refining content strategy.

Vercel Edge CDN

Sub-50ms TTFB globally. Preview deploys per branch. Automatically achieves LCP < 2.5s on fast connections from day one.
